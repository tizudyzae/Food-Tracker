# Mounjaro Food & Weight Logging Rules

Updated 16 August 2026. This replaces the earlier three-rule sheet.

## Purpose and default targets

- Keep one food log per calendar day, recording calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fibre and salt whenever the data is available.
- Default daily calorie range: **2,000 to 2,200 kcal**. Treat 2,200 kcal as the normal ceiling unless Nathan explicitly sets a different plan for that day.
- Protein is important, but not a target to force: **150 to 170 g/day is already ample**. Prefer vegetables, fibre and food volume rather than extra lean protein once this is covered.
- Flag unusually high salt, very low fibre, or a high-fat meal where it might affect Mounjaro tolerance. Do not present a normal one-off meal as a failure.
- Track Mounjaro tolerance only when symptoms are reported. Important symptoms to note: nausea, vomiting, reflux, constipation, diarrhoea, dizziness, weakness and inability to keep fluids down.

## What counts as logged

- Log food only when Nathan clearly says he has eaten/is eating it **and gives a time**.
- A proposed meal, shopping photo or nutritional discussion is not a log entry. Clearly label it as proposed.
- Use the actual time supplied, including a backdated time. If no time is supplied, ask for one rather than silently logging it.
- Food eaten after midnight as part of the late evening routine stays with the day that began before midnight, unless Nathan says it is a new day. State this explicitly in the final daily total.
- Corrections replace the earlier entry. Do not count the old and new version together.
- For packaged foods in the reference database below, no new photo is needed unless the pack size, flavour, recipe or label has changed.
- For foods not in the database, or where the weight/amount differs, ask for a label photo or the exact quantity. Never invent a precise number.

## Usual timing pattern

These are reference windows, not required meal times:

| Typical eating point | Usual time |
|---|---|
| First meal | 14:00 to 14:30, average about 14:11 |
| Work/evening meal or shakes | 19:30 to 20:15 |
| Main late stir-fry | 23:30 to 00:30, average about 00:24 |

The late meal is often the largest meal, roughly 950 kcal on average. Meal timing is informational only: total intake and Mounjaro tolerance matter more.

### Common meal templates

- **Standard chicken stir-fry:** weighed chicken breast, one whole rice/noodle pack, vegetables and one sauce pouch. Always confirm the chicken weight and exact rice/noodle/sauce variant.
- **Quick work meal:** a Huel/UFIT/other protein drink plus chicken chunks, chips or prawn crackers and sometimes a banana. Use the database portions above.
- When a whole pack is used, log it as the listed pack. When a partial pack is used, calculate proportionally only where the matching pack data is known.

## Daily reporting standard

After each confirmed entry, provide the itemised meal and updated running total. At day end provide:

1. calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre and salt, marked estimated where appropriate;
2. remaining calories against 2,000 and 2,200 kcal, or confirmation that the day is complete;
3. a short, level-headed note on protein, fibre, salt and Mounjaro tolerance only where relevant;
4. any missing label/portion details that prevent a reliable total.

## Weight tracking

- Add a weigh-in at least weekly, ideally under comparable conditions: morning, after the toilet, before food/drink, similar clothing.
- Focus on the rolling trend, not isolated changes. Food salt, late meals and hydration can move scale weight sharply for a few days.
- Current supplied reference: **126.4 kg on 29 July 2026**.

## Maintenance rules

- When a new packaged food is confirmed from a clear label, add the product, exact portion and full available label nutrition to this sheet.
- If a manufacturer changes a recipe or a pack size, replace the prior entry rather than averaging the two.
- Keep unknown values blank. Better a clear gap than pretend precision.
